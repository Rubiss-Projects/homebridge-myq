Connect myQ with your iPhone

Sign into the official myQ app once. Homebridge saves the credentials privately and renews them automatically while myQ permits the session.

This guided setup temporarily intercepts myQ sign-in on your trusted home Wi-Fi. You approve a certificate on the iPhone, then remove the temporary profile. It also expires automatically after 15 minutes. Door controls in this plugin are paused until setup ends.

For WPA/WPA2/WPA3 personal networks. Leave the password blank to enter it on the iPhone if prompted. Wi-Fi credentials are used only in the temporary profile and are not saved to plugin configuration.

Setup stays active for 15 minutes even if you switch apps. Scan with your iPhone Camera, or open the setup link in Safari if you are already on the phone.

Open iPhone setup

After signing in, remove myQ Temporary Setup under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Verify your normal Wi-Fi connection, then finish here. If setup times out remove the profile before trying again.
